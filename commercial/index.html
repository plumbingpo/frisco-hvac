Kitchen Climate Control

Climate Control for Kitchens, Restaurants, and Food Facilities

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and a system that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Cooking lines dump huge heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air out as fast as the rooftop units can make it. Our food-service work covers rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, with service scheduled around your hours.

Comfort and air balance are critical issues for any food-service operator. A kitchen running negative pressure pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags outdoor air through every door, and makes staff miserable during peak service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and airflow your operation depends on, with documented service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to landlords and auditors. You get detailed reports and service logs after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work around your business, not the other way around. We coordinate every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and disruption stays minimal so the kitchen keeps cooking and the dining room stays comfortable.

Inspections & Documentation

Safe Installations, Documented Service

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. We inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify proper venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Consistent professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming emergencies. It also builds a paper trail. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. That record shows landlords, insurers, and auditors that you've done your due diligence.

Rapid Response

Rapid-Response HVAC Repair for Businesses

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens overheat, retail floors empty out, server rooms heat up, and tenants start calling. Calls to our commercial line get a technician rolling quickly, throughout Frisco and the surrounding area.

Our technicians are equipped to handle any commercial HVAC emergency, including failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, and control board lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring heating or cooling as quickly as possible, then put maintenance and monitoring in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records and facility files.

Maintenance Programs

Proactive HVAC Care for Businesses

Our commercial maintenance program is built around your equipment, how the space is used, and your budget. On each scheduled visit, we handle inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes at the right MERV rating, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep before peak demand.
